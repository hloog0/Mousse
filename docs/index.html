What it does

Glides, doesn't jump

Each wheel notch becomes a short, eased animation, and a spinning wheel becomes one continuous glide.

Stops when you stop

"Glide after you stop" sets how quickly scrolling settles, from a crisp stop to a long coast.

Speeds up when you spin

Fast spins take bigger steps, so long pages are still quick to get through.

Leaves your trackpad alone

Trackpad and Magic Mouse gestures pass straight through untouched.

Off where you want it off

Turn smoothing off in specific apps, or turn it off entirely, from the menu bar.

Private and tiny

No accounts, no network access, nothing collected. It's a small menu bar app with no Dock icon.

Install

  1. Download SmoothWheel.zip and double-click it to unzip.
  2. Drag SmoothWheel into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it. When macOS says it couldn't verify the app, click Done. Then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.
  4. Turn on SmoothWheel under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, then click the mouse icon in the menu bar to adjust it.

Why the warning? Apple charges developers a yearly fee to sign apps so macOS opens them without asking. SmoothWheel is free, so it isn't signed that way. Its full source code is on GitHub, so you can read it or build it yourself.

Questions

Which mice does it work with?

Any mouse with a notched scroll wheel. It was built for and tested with the Logitech Signature M750. If your mouse software has its own smooth scrolling option, turn that off so the two don't stack.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

That's the only way macOS lets an app intercept scroll events and replace them with smooth ones. SmoothWheel only listens to scroll-wheel events. It never sees keystrokes or clicks and never sends anything anywhere.

Is it really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT license: free to use, share and modify.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from its menu bar panel, drag it from Applications to the Trash, and remove it from System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
